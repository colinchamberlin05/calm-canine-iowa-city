Dog daycare for anxious dogs Iowa City

A slower first visit is still progress.

Some dogs need time before they can enjoy a group setting. Calm Canine’s concept is to make the decision observable: ask better questions, introduce carefully, and adjust the plan when a dog is overwhelmed.

1. Learn the signals

We ask about body language, past care, triggers, recovery time, and what helps at home.

2. Start small

A short, quiet introduction gives the dog a chance to explore without being rushed into a busy group.

3. Share what happened

An owner update explains appetite, energy, play, rest, and the next reasonable step.

Questions to ask any facility

  • What happens when a dog needs space?
  • How are first visits supervised?
  • What does an owner update include?
  • Can the visit be shortened if the dog is overwhelmed?
