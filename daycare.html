Dog daycare Iowa City

A daycare rhythm built around play and rest.

Calm Canine is designed for owners who want more detail than a generic “let them play” promise. Dogs get supervised activity, quiet breaks, and owner updates.

Temperament consult

We start by learning about your dog’s routine, comfort signals, play style, and triggers.

Small first introduction

The first visit is slower and more controlled than a typical drop-off.

Planned rest

Rest periods protect dogs from becoming overstimulated and help owners understand the day.

Before you book

  • Bring vaccination records and a current emergency contact.
  • Share feeding, medication, and behavior details honestly.
  • Ask how the team handles a dog that needs a break.
  • Start with a shorter visit if your dog is new to group care.
