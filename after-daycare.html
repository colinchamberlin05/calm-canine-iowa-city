Why is my dog tired after daycare?

Rest can be part of a good day.

New smells, play, social decisions, and a different routine can leave a dog tired after daycare. The useful question is how the dog recovers and whether anything else seems unusual.

Usually reassuring

Drinking, eating normally, relaxing, and returning to normal energy after a good rest can be reassuring signs.

Ask questions

Ask for the day’s schedule, activity level, appetite, and any behavior the team noticed.

Seek professional help

If your dog seems unwell, has concerning symptoms, or does not recover as expected, contact a veterinarian.

Use the update to plan the next visit

A useful report card should help you decide whether to repeat the same length of visit, add more rest, or ask for a slower introduction next time.
